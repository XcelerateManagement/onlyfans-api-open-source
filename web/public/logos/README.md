# Provider brand marks

Source: [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) — the SVG files are released
under **CC0 1.0** (public domain), which is why they are safe to vendor here
rather than scraping marks off each company's site.

The **icons** are CC0. The **brands** are still trademarks of their owners.
Usage rules that apply regardless of the file licence:

- Use them descriptively — "deploy to Railway" — never in a way that implies
  Railway endorses, sponsors or partners with us.
- Do not put a provider mark next to our logo in a lock-up.
- Do not recolour a mark in a way its brand guidelines forbid. These are
  single-path monochrome marks and inherit `currentColor`, which is the
  intended use.
- Keep them out of OG images and ads, where implied endorsement is harder to
  argue.

Refresh a mark:

    curl -o public/logos/<slug>.svg \
      https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@latest/icons/<slug>.svg

Slugs in use: hetzner, digitalocean, railway, render, flydotio, coolify,
caddy, docker, openai, claude, googlegemini, cursor, netcup, ovh,
contabo, vultr, linode, koyeb.
